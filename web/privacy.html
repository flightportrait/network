Feeder privacy.

Draft, September 2026. What the network keeps about a station and a visitor. The network is operated by YODE PTE LTD, Singapore. The frame, the app and the shop have their own privacy policy.

1. What a station sends.

Aircraft messages and the station key you chose. Aircraft messages are public broadcasts, not personal data. The key is the network's only identity for a station. No account, no name, no email.

2. What we keep.

A one-way hash of the key, never the key. The area the station hears, computed from its traffic and rounded to about 11 km. First and last heard, online or not, message rate.

3. What we do not keep.

Your IP address holds the connection open and is discarded when it closes. We never ask for or publish an address or exact coordinates. MLAT uses your antenna position in its calculation, only while the station is connected, and never publishes it.

4. What is public.

The roster: a generated identifier, the rounded area, the dates, online or not. The aggregate of every station's data is open data under ODbL 1.0, as the feeder terms say.

5. Visitors to the map.

The map is static files talking to our API. We hold IP address and browser type briefly for security and rate limiting, and nothing for analytics or advertising. Aircraft photographs load from planespotters.net and map tiles from OpenFreemap, in your browser, under their terms.

6. The Station software.

Its setup page runs in your browser on your own network. It asks OpenFreemap for tiles, Nominatim when you type an address, Open-Meteo for the ground elevation at the pin, and ipwho.is for a rough first position. None of that reaches us.

7. Deletion and questions.

Email hello@flightportrait.com with the station key to delete a station's history. Data already published under ODbL stays under its license. Data protection questions go to privacy@flightportrait.com.
